10-Stage Vertical Technical Flow

From Sustainable Raw Fiber to Global Sea Dispatch.

Every garment moves through a synchronized 10-point quality pipeline with real-time RFID tracking, ISO testing, and AQL 1.5 defect audits.

STEP-BY-STEP WORKFLOW

10 Precision Manufacturing Stages

End-to-end traceability guaranteeing compliance with EU CSDDD and US Customs UFLPA chain-of-custody documentation.

Stage 01 • Inward Testing

Sustainable Fiber & Yarn Sourcing

Procurement of 100% GOTS organic cotton, BCI combed yarns, Lenzing modal, and GRS recycled polyester. Every incoming yarn lot undergoes automated Uster testing for yarn count consistency, twist per inch (TPI), and zero foreign fiber contamination.

Uster Evenness Tester • Contamination < 0.01%

All yarn lots digitally logged with farm origin transaction certificates.

Stage 02 • Digital Colorimetry

Digital Lab Dips & Spectrophotometer Matching

Color recipes formulated using computerized dispensing units and tested with Datacolor 800 spectrophotometers under D65, TL84, and CWF light sources. Lab dips dispatched internationally within 48 to 72 hours for buyer signoff.

Delta E < 0.5 • 48-Hour Dispatch

Complete spectral curve report generated for batch-to-batch shade matching.

Stage 03 • Pattern Engineering

Gerber CAD Pattern Grading & 3D Fit Validation

Translating tech packs into computerized production markers using Gerber AccuMark 3D virtual avatar simulation. Full size run grading (XS to 4XL) with optimized fabric utilization reaching 94%.

Virtual 3D Avatar Fit • 94% Marker Yield

Eliminates multiple courier rounds of physical sample prototyping.

Stage 04 • Laser & CNC Cutting

Automated Multi-Ply Vacuum CNC Knife Cutting

Precision reciprocating knife cutting with automatic blade sharpening and computerized vacuum hold-down tables. Slices up to 70 plies simultaneously with zero ply distortion.

Tolerance ±0.5mm • 140,000 Yards Daily

Thermal ply numbering ensures 100% shade matching across all panels.

Stage 05 • Modular Lean Assembly

Assembly on 45 Modular Lean Sewing Lines

Over 1,800 programmable Juki DDL-9000C direct-drive lockstitch, 4-thread overlock, and flatlock machines configured in single-piece progressive flow. Hourly line balance audits maintain 78%–84% SAM efficiency.

150,000 Daily Units • RFID Bundle Tracking

Live digital Andon system alerts supervisors to any tension deviation.

Stage 06 • Surface Embellishment

Tajima Multi-Head Embroidery & Screen Printing

32-head Tajima embroidery carousels and automatic 16-color M&R screen printing carousels executing high-density silicone prints, water-based discharge inks, and metallic accents with OEKO-TEX Class 1 compliance.

25,000 Daily Impressions • Wash Fastness Grade 4-5

Tested to withstand 50+ commercial washing cycles without cracking.

Stage 07 • Wash & Finish

Industrial Garment Bio-Wash & Softening

Industrial Tonello and Tolkar front-loading washing drums executing enzyme bio-polishing, silicon softener dips, vintage pigment dyeing, and ozone dry fading with zero hazardous chemical effluents.

Zero-Shrinkage Compaction • Silk Hand Feel

Residual dimensional change controlled to strictly under ±3%.

Stage 08 • Quality Assurance

Multi-Tier In-Line & Final AQL 1.5 Inspection

Certified quality auditors inspect every garment against the approved golden sample and tech pack measurement chart. Critical dimensions, SPI count, symmetry, and barcode accuracy verified before packing.

AQL 1.5 Level II Sampling Standard

Zero-tolerance for loose threads, skipped stitches, or color banding.

Stage 09 • Retail Finishing

Vacuum Steam Ironing, Barcode Hangtag & Polybagging

Veit industrial form finishers provide sharp crease definitions and wrinkle-free silhouettes. Garments pass through Hashima dual-head metal detector tunnels before polybagging in GRS-certified biodegradable bags.

100% Metal Detector Scanned (0.8mm Ferrous)

Automated polybag sealing with printed GS1-128 compliance barcodes.

Stage 10 • Maritime Export

Pallet Staging & Direct Sea Container Stuffing

Finished export cartons stacked on heat-treated ISPM-15 wooden pallets with moisture desiccant bags and stretch film. Direct factory container docks load 20-ft and 40-ft high-cube containers for transit to international seaports.

Chennai & Tuticorin Ports • 4-6 Hours Transit

Digital e-Bill of Lading and customs export clearance dossier provided.
