FACILITY DIVISIONS

World-Class Machinery & Production Lines

Combining heavy industrial throughput with agile modular cells to deliver both massive 500,000-unit wholesale orders and rapid capsule collections.

Division 01 • Cutting Hall

Computerized CAD Grading & Multi-Ply CNC Cutting

Minimizing fabric wastage and guaranteeing absolute ply-to-ply sizing conformity across high-volume production.

Our cutting room operates 6 high-speed automatic spreading machines equipped with tension-free edge alignment alongside 4 Gerber Paragon automated multi-ply CNC cutters. We process over 140,000 yards of fabric daily under vacuum compression, achieving up to 94% marker utilization efficiency.

Gerber AccuMark 3D

Digital marker grading & nesting optimization.

Thermal QR Bundling

Traceable RFID bundle tags for every cut ply.

Division 02 • Sewing Lines

45 Modular Lean Sewing Lines with Juki & Brother Automats

Configured for single-piece flow, rapid line changeover, and zero work-in-progress bottlenecks.

Our sewing halls house over 1,800 computerized direct-drive lockstitch, 4-thread overlock, and cylinder flatlock stations. With real-time RFID bundle scanning and digital Andon displays, line supervisors track unit outputs and detect stitch anomalies in real time.

Juki DDL-9000C Automats

Digital micro-pitch needle feed & automatic thread trimming.

SAM Line Balancing

78% - 84% Standard Allowed Minutes line efficiency.

Division 03 • Embellishment Studio

Computerized Tajima Embroidery & Automated M&R Printing

Intricate surface embellishments executed with eco-certified reactive inks and high-speed multi-head precision.

From high-density silicone prints and discharge inks to metallic foil and 3D puff embroidery, our embellishment studio operates 16-color automatic M&R screen printing carousels alongside high-resolution Kornit Vulcan direct-to-garment systems and 20-head Tajima embroidery stations.

Tajima 20-Head Units

Laser appliqué positioning & metallic thread tensioning.

OEKO-TEX Class 1 Inks

Formaldehyde-free water-based reactive pigments.

Division 04 • Knitting & Dyeing

Mayer & Cie Circular Knitting & Soft-Flow Eco-Dyeing

Custom knit development from 120 GSM single jerseys to 450 GSM luxury loopback French terry fabrics.

Our knitting plant houses 40 high-speed German Mayer & Cie circular knitting machines producing single jerseys, interlock, pique, rib, and fleece. Our adjoining dye house features low-liquor ratio Fong’s soft-flow dyeing vessels connected directly to our biological Zero Liquid Discharge plant.

Gauges 18 to 36

Ultra-fine gauges for silky drape and compact knit structure.

1:4 Low Liquor Ratio

Drastically reduced thermal energy and water consumption.

Division 05 • Quality Lab

ISO 17025 Accredited In-House Fabric & Chemical Testing Lab

Full physical and chemical analytical capability before any fabric roll touches the cutting tables.

Our dedicated testing facility is equipped with Datacolor spectrophotometers for digital lab dips, Martindale abrasion testers, Crocking meters, and automated shrinkage washers. We issue full technical inspection dossiers with every commercial export dispatch.

Delta E < 0.5 Precision

Computerized color shade matching under D65 & TL84 light.

Crocking & Pilling Grade 4-5

Tested to strict ISO 105-X12 and ASTM D3512 benchmarks.

Division 06 • Bonded Logistics

Veit Steam Tunnel Pressing & Bonded Maritime Warehousing

Pristine retail-ready garment presentation and direct sea-container stuffing on factory campus.

Our packaging floor operates 12 Veit industrial steam formers, automated barcode tagging systems, and metal detection gates. Finished cartons are staged in our 60,000 sq. ft. high-bay palletized warehouse with direct 40-foot high-cube container docks for sea transit to Rotterdam, Hamburg, Los Angeles, and Felixstowe.

9-Point Needle Detector

Hashima dual-head metal detection for zero hazardous foreign elements.

Port Transit: 4-6 Hours

Direct highway corridor to Chennai & Tuticorin international seaports.
