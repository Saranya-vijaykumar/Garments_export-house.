SURFACE VALUE ADDITION

Artisanal Detailing Scaled To Industrial Export Volumes

At Garmen, surface embellishment is treated with sartorial mastery. Our multi-head Tajima embroidery suite, automated MHM screen printing carousels, and Tonello bio-wash centers empower international fashion houses to deliver signature tactile treatments that withstand 50+ commercial wash cycles.

Every ink formulation, embroidery backing, and metal trim adheres strictly to ZDHC Level 3, OEKO-TEX Standard 100 Class 1 (safe for babywear), and EU REACH safety guidelines with non-toxic, skin-friendly ingredients.

SIGNATURE TREATMENTS

Embellishment Formats & Finishing Techniques

Tailored finishes spanning streetwear graphics, high-fashion embroidery, and eco-certified garment washes.

Computerized 3D Puff Embroidery

High-density 2mm to 4mm EVA foam core embroidery creating tactile relief logos, chenille loop patches, and metallic filament monograms.

Water-Based & Discharge Screen Print

Zero-handfeel breathable printing that dyes the fiber itself. Perfect for large dark garments without heavy plastic ink deposits.

High-Density Silicone & Heat Transfers

3D molded liquid silicone badges with razor-sharp beveled edges. Unmatched elasticity that never cracks on 4-way stretch activewear.

Enzyme Bio-Polish & Vintage Mineral Wash

Cellulase enzyme treatment eliminating micro-fuzz for silky peach-skin handfeel, combined with ozone bleaching to save 85% water.

Natural Corozo & Shell Button Packaging

Sustainable hardware including laser-engraved tagua palm corozo buttons, recycled metal snaps, and biodegradable zipper pullers.

FSC Kraft Hangtags & Compostable Bags

Retail-ready export packaging featuring FSC certified 400 GSM kraft cardstock, vegetable soy ink barcodes, and home-compostable polybags.
