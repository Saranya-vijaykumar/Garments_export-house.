SYNCHRONIZED MANUFACTURING

Lean Sewing Engineering & High-Velocity Stitching

Garmen's stitching plant combines Toyota Lean Manufacturing principles with next-generation Japanese digital sewing workstations. Our 28 dedicated assembly lines run synchronized overhead garment hanger systems, delivering cut panels straight to each operator's workstation with computerized takt-time pacing.

Equipped with direct-drive digital feed mechanisms, our machines dynamically adjust thread tension on-the-fly to handle delicate microfiber knits, heavyweight 420 GSM fleece, or rigid denim seams without puckering, distortion, or seam slippage.

STITCHING CAPABILITIES

Specialized Seaming & Automated Profiling

Every seam class certified to ISO 4915 international stitch geometry and ASTM D1683 seam strength benchmarks.

ISO 301 Lockstitch Precision

Computer-controlled needle feed preventing fabric puckering on ultra-fine woven shirts, silk blouses, and formal tailoring.

ISO 504 & 514 Overlock / Mock Safety

High-speed 7,000 RPM differential feed overlockers ensuring stretch elasticity for tees, hoodies, and stretch activewear.

Flatlock & Activewear Flatseam

4-needle, 6-thread zero-abrasion flatlock seams designed for chafeless compression apparel, yoga leggings, and rash guards.

Automated Pocket Setters & Welting

Computerized Juki APW units that laser-cut, fold, and sew single or double welt pockets in under 12 seconds with sub-millimeter symmetry.

Multi-Head Buttonholing & Attaching

Programmable electronic eyelet buttonholers and indexing button sewers equipped with optical thread cutters and index counters.

100% Inline Metal Detection & QA

Lockout conveyor needle detectors sensitive down to 0.8mm ferrous particles, ensuring zero needle contamination in export shipments.
