International Trade Framework

Incoterms 2020 Export Protocols

We routinely support international shipping agreements across all standard international commercial shipping terms.

FOB (Free On Board)

We manage factory production, inland transport, and loading past the ship rail at origin port. Buyer controls ocean freight and destination customs.

CIF (Cost, Insurance & Freight)

GlobalStitch pays marine insurance and ocean freight to your nominated port of destination (e.g. Rotterdam, Hamburg, NY). Buyer handles import customs.

CFR (Cost & Freight)

We cover factory production, origin customs, and ocean freight to the destination port, while the buyer arranges their own cargo insurance policy.

DDP (Delivered Duty Paid)

Complete door-to-door turnkey fulfillment. We handle shipping, customs clearance, import tariffs, and final truck delivery straight to your distribution warehouse.
